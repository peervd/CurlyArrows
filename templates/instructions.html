How to Use CurlyArrows

Getting Started

  1. Select an exercise from the 'Exercises' menu and read the question
  2. Use the ChemDoodle sketcher to draw the reaction mechanism
    • Write out the mechanism from left to right
    • If a resonance structure is present, draw it below the corresponding molecule in the main chain
    • Use curved arrows to show electron movement
    • Indicate formal charges on atoms
    • For proton transfer steps: write out specific protons ‘H’ and their bond in the molecular structure
    • You can use 'H+' for and acid and 'Ba' for a base
  3. Use the textbox for your reasoning:
    • For each step identify whether it is a ‘proton transfer’, ‘nucleophilic attack’, ‘loss of a leaving group’, ‘rearrangement’ or a combination
    • Explain why each step occurs
    • Example: Step 1 is a proton transfer. Here, a proton is abstracted from the acidic alpha carbon next to the carbonyl. Hydroxide, [OH-], acts as a strong base. Step 2 is a …
  4. Click "ANALYZE" to get AI-powered feedback

Feedback System

The AI analyzes your mechanism based on:

  • Molecular structure accuracy
  • Correctness of the transformations
  • Logical flow of electrons (curved arrows)
  • Your written reasoning
